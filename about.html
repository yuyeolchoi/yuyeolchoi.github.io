Research profile

About

A concise account of my research background, established expertise, and published contributions.

I am a Ph.D. candidate in chemistry at Chonnam National University. My research focuses on heterogeneous catalysis for C1 conversion and hydrogen production, with particular attention to methane and CO2 activation, catalytic dehydrogenation, and hydrogen carrier systems.

I combine experimental catalysis with DFT-based descriptor and mechanism analysis using tools such as ORCA and Quantum ESPRESSO. Across my published work, I have studied zeolite and metal oxide catalysts, metal-support interactions, catalyst descriptors, and structure-performance relationships.

This portfolio documents completed and publicly available scholarship. For publication details, see the publications page; for training and professional experience, see my curriculum vitae.
